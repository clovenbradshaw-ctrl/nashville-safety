These are verbatim extracts from the traffic crash sections of each annual report PDF.
Note: No 2012 Annual Report PDF is listed at the reports index page.

--- 2008 Annual Report (https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/13-2008.pdf) ---
Verbatim from 2011 report citing 2008: "In 2008 there were 67 fatal crashes with 72 deaths; 15 of those occurred on interstate systems."

--- 2010 Annual Report (https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/11-2010.pdf) ---
"In 2010, 74 persons died in 70 fatal crashes. Motor vehicle crash-related deaths increased (8.8%), and the number of fatal crashes increased 9.4%."

--- 2011 Annual Report (https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/10-2011.pdf) ---
Table (verbatim): "Motor vehicle crash-related deaths increased by 1.4% and fatal crashes increased by 7.1% from 2010 to 2011. [Table:] 2010 Deaths 74 / Fatal Crashes 70. 2011 Deaths 75 / Fatal Crashes 75. Total 144 deaths / 150 crashes."

--- 2013 Annual Report (https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/8-2013.pdf) ---
"In 2013, 68 persons died in 64 fatal crashes. As a result, fatal crashes decreased by 3% and crash-related deaths increased by 1.5% from 2012-2013."
"Homicide was at its lowest level in the 51-year history of the Metropolitan Government."
"Homicides, Burglary and Motor Vehicle Theft were at their lowest rate since history of the Metropolitan Government in 1963."

--- 2014 Annual Report (https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/7-2014.pdf) ---
"In 2014, 64 persons died in 63 fatal crashes. As a result, fatal crashes decreased by 1.6% and crash-related deaths decreased by 5.9% from 2013 to 2014."
"Not only was the number of crash-related deaths at a 23 year low, but it was at the second lowest level in the history of the Metropolitan Government."
"1966 was the year with the highest number of persons killed in motor vehicle crashes with a total of 129."

--- 2015 Annual Report (https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/6-2015.pdf) ---
"In 2015, 75 persons died in 69 fatal crashes. As a result, fatal crashes increased by 9.5% and crash-related deaths increased by 17.2% from 2014 to 2015."
"Nashville's growth, with more vehicles on our roadways, contributed to a 17.2% increase in crash-related deaths in 2015 (64 to 75)."
"1966 was the year with the highest number of persons killed in motor vehicle crashes with a total of 129."
"Criminal Homicide demonstrated the largest percent of increase at 92.7%."

--- 2016 Annual Report (https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/5-2016.pdf) ---
"Sixty-seven persons died in fatal crashes during 2016, a 10.7% reduction from 2015."
"In 2016, 67 persons died in 65 fatal crashes. This constituted a decrease of 5.8% in fatal crashes and a decrease of 10.7% in crash-related deaths from 2015 to 2016."
"1966 was the year with the highest number of persons killed in motor vehicle crashes with a total of 129."
"Robbery was up in 2016 by 5.7%, homicide increased 5.1%, and aggravated assault showed a slight increase at 0.2%."

--- 2017 Annual Report (https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/4-2017.pdf) ---
"In 2017, 75 persons died in 74 fatal crashes. That results in a 11.9% increase in persons killed in crashes and a 13.8% increase in the number of fatal crashes."
"2014 was the year with the lowest number of persons killed in motor vehicle crashes with a total of 64."
"Criminal homicide increased 33.7%, aggravated assault increased 2.8%, robbery had a 2.6% increase, larceny increased 2.4%, and rape increased slightly at 0.3%."
