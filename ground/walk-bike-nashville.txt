From "2016–2020 Pedestrian Fatality Trends" (published Jan. 27, 2021):
URL: https://www.walkbikenashville.org/2016_2020_pedestrian_fatality_trends
"Just ten years ago in 2010 there were 14 people killed while walking. Last year in 2020, Nashville lost 39 pedestrians -- nearly triple the amount."

From "Understanding Pedestrian Crash Trends" (published Dec. 11, 2020):
URL: https://www.walkbikenashville.org/understanding_pedestrian_crash_trends
"In 2019, the average speed limit on a road where a pedestrian was killed was 43 mph, a speed at which, if hit, a person has a 90% chance of dying."
"These crashes are not happening randomly. In fact they can largely be explained by the poor design of our streets."
"42% of deaths involved crossing outside crosswalks."
"76% of 2019 pedestrian deaths occurred after dark; 47% of those in poorly-lit or unlit areas."

From Vision Zero 2023 review:
URL: https://www.walkbikenashville.org/vision_zero_2023
"59 percent of all fatal crashes occur on just 6 percent of roads."
"60 percent of fatalities of people walking occur on just 2 percent of roads."

Pedestrian fatality counts (from Walk Bike Nashville / WPLN):
2008: 13
2010: 14
2016: 15
2018: 22
2019: 33
2020: 39
2021: 39
2022: 49 (WPLN: "all-time high for pedestrian fatalities")
