# MNPD / Nashville: violent crime vs. road safety vs. enforcement

Purpose: one index of facts, each pointing to the bytes it came from, so the three trendlines
can be compared: (A) violent crime, (B) road deaths, (C) roadside stops by police.

**Status of C: no traffic-stop data is in this folder.** Nothing below measures how many stops
MNPD makes. Do not infer a stop trend from A or B. See section C at the end.

## Reading A and B side by side (what the bytes support, nothing more)
Violent count peaked 2022 (9,060) and fell in 2023-2024 (CNT). Recorded road deaths peaked 2023
(136) and were 116 in 2024 (CRASH). Pedestrian deaths were 24-41 a year in 2017-2020 and 24-44 in 2021-2025, with no
clear step (CRASH). Those are separate series, not a cause. Nothing here says whether stops
changed, because no stops series is present.
